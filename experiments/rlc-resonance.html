Theory Recap

RLC series circuit, output taken across \(R\) — band-pass filter:

$$\underline{H}(\omega) = \frac{jx/Q}{1 - x^2 + jx/Q} \qquad x = \frac{\omega}{\omega_0} \qquad \omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} \qquad Q = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}$$
RLC band-pass transfer function

At resonance \(\omega = \omega_0\) (\(x = 1\)): \(|\underline{H}| = 1\) (0 dB) — the reactive components cancel. The bandwidth is:

$$\Delta f = f_2 - f_1 = \frac{f_0}{Q} = \frac{R}{2\pi L} \qquad f_1 f_2 = f_0^2$$
bandwidth — high Q gives narrow resonance
