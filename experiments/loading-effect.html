Loading Effect

How \(f_c\) shifts as load resistance \(R_L\) changes. Verify the theoretical \(f_c(R_L)\) relation and observe the Bode diagram at a fixed load.

Theory L4 · Loading Effect L1 · Voltage Divider L5 · Follower L3 · Impedance

Theory Recap

The unloaded CR high-pass has \(f_c = 1/(2\pi RC)\). When a load \(R_L\) is placed across the output, the effective resistance becomes \(R \| R_L\) and the transfer function changes:

$$\underline{H}'(\omega) = \frac{R \| R_L}{R \| R_L + \underline{Z}_C} \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad \boxed{f_c(R_L) = \frac{R + R_L}{2\pi C \cdot R \cdot R_L}}$$
loaded CR high-pass — cutoff frequency shifts with \(R_L\)

As \(R_L \to \infty\) (no load): \(f_c \to 1/(2\pi RC)\) — the unloaded value. As \(R_L \to 0\): \(f_c \to \infty\) — the filter is destroyed. The smaller the load, the higher the cutoff frequency and the more distorted the passband.
