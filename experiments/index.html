Experiments

Interactive lab notebooks — enter measurements, compute derived quantities, compare to theory. Data saved in LocalStorage; import/export as CSV.

Data is stored locally in your browser. Use the ↓ CSV button on each page to back up your measurements. Use ↑ Import to restore or load converted TP data. The converter script is at tools/csv-convert.py.
RC / RL First-Order Filter
Bode table, \(g_{dB}\) computation, \(f_c\) finder at \(-3\,\text{dB}\). Compare measured vs theoretical \(f_c = 1/(2\pi RC)\) or \(R/(2\pi L)\).
Loading Effect
Measure \(f_c\) vs \(R_L\). Plot \(f_c(R_L)\) and compare to the theoretical curve. Quantify how load shifts the cutoff frequency.
Cascaded Filters
CR-CR (order 2 high-pass) and CR-RC (band-pass). Compare \(-40\,\text{dB/dec}\) slope vs \(-20\,\text{dB/dec}\) for a single stage.
RLC Resonance
Determine \(\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC}\), quality factor \(Q\), bandwidth \(\Delta f = f_0/Q\). Observe surtension when \(Q > 1/\sqrt{2}\).
Active Filter (Op-Amp)
Bode diagram with \(G_v > 1\). Verify \(f_c\) is load-independent. Compare active vs passive filter characteristics.
