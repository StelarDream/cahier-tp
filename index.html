UE 404 · EC 404.1

Cahier d'Électrocinétique

A ground-up theory roadmap — from charge and potential through passive filters and op-amp circuits.

How to use this

Each layer is self-contained. Work through them in order the first time; during the exam, jump directly to whatever you need via the sidebar. Every quantity is introduced with its rank, unit, and SI dimension vector. Every law is stated in its full differential form first, then simplified for the circuit context.

Layer Map

L0
Voltage & Current
What charge, potential, and current actually are. From the Coulomb field to \(U = V_A - V_B\).
L1
Kirchhoff Laws
KCL and KVL as the complete rules for node/branch analysis. The voltage divider.
L2
Component Laws
Constitutive relations for R, C, L in differential form. Why C blocks DC and L blocks HF.
L3
Complex Impedance
\(\partial_t \to j\omega\). How sinusoidal regime collapses ODEs into algebra. \(Z_C, Z_L, Z_R\).
L4
Filters & Bode
Transfer function \(H(\omega)\), gain, phase, cutoff at \(-3\,\text{dB}\). RC, RL, RLC. Bode diagrams.
L5
Op-Amp Circuits
The two golden rules. Inverting, non-inverting, follower, integrator, differentiator, active filters.
